Privacy

Data Deletion Request

You may request deletion of your Dingfeng Creator Studio account and associated TikTok-related information at any time.

Submit a request

  1. Email privacy@dingfengchuangyu.com.
  2. Use the email address registered with Dingfeng Creator Studio.
  3. Use the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  4. Include your account email address and TikTok display name.
  5. Do not send your password or TikTok access token.

What happens next

After verifying account ownership, we will acknowledge the request within 7 business days and normally complete deletion within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

The deletion process includes

  • Deleting or invalidating stored TikTok authorization tokens;
  • Deleting TikTok profile information stored by the service;
  • Deleting uploaded videos and publishing records, subject to legal requirements; and
  • Closing the related Dingfeng Creator Studio account.

You can also disconnect TikTok within Creator Studio or revoke access through your TikTok account settings.
