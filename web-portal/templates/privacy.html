Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026
Last Updated: August 6, 2026

This policy explains how {{ company_name }} processes information when you use Dingfeng Creator Studio at {{ domain }}.

1. Information we process

  • TikTok OAuth identifiers, access and refresh tokens, granted scopes and expiration information.
  • The connected creator nickname, username, temporary avatar URL and publishing capabilities.
  • The video file, caption, privacy choice, interaction settings and commercial-content declarations you submit.
  • Publishing identifiers, processing status, errors, basic security logs and device/browser information.

2. Why we use it

We use this information only to authenticate your account, display the correct publishing controls, transfer content at your request, show processing status, prevent abuse and maintain service security.

3. Video handling

Uploaded video files are held temporarily while they are transferred to TikTok and are deleted from our upload directory after the transfer attempt. We do not use creator videos to train models, create advertising profiles or sell content to third parties.

4. Token storage and separation

OAuth tokens are stored server-side and associated with the user's service session. They are not placed in public page source and are not shared with other creators. Access is limited to the functions required to provide the requested TikTok integration.

5. Sharing

Content and publishing settings are sent to TikTok only after your explicit action. We may disclose limited information to infrastructure providers or authorities when required to operate securely or comply with law. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Temporary upload files are deleted after transfer. OAuth records are kept while your account remains connected or as needed for security and legal obligations. Use the Disconnect control or email us to request deletion.

7. Your choices

You may decline authorization, disconnect your TikTok account, revoke access through TikTok, or request access, correction or deletion by visiting our Data Deletion page or contacting privacy@dingfengchuangyu.com.
