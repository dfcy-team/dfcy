About the service

A publishing workspace built around creator choice.

Dingfeng Creator Studio is operated by {{ company_name }} (厦门市鼎峰创域科技有限公司).

What the service does

Eligible independent creators can connect a TikTok account they own or are authorized to manage, prepare original video content, review available publishing options and send that content to TikTok through official APIs.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok OAuth.
  2. The service retrieves the latest creator profile and available publishing settings.
  3. The creator selects and previews a video, edits the caption and chooses all available settings.
  4. Nothing is sent until the creator gives explicit consent and starts the transfer.
  5. The service displays TikTok's processing and publishing status.

Who can use it

The publishing workspace is intended for a broad audience of authentic creators. It is not restricted to Dingfeng employees, company-managed accounts or a private internal group.

Creator control

We do not choose a creator's account, add watermarks, prefill promotional captions or publish without explicit user action. Users can disconnect TikTok at any time and request deletion of their related information.

Dingfeng Creator Studio is an independent service. It is not owned, operated or endorsed by TikTok.
