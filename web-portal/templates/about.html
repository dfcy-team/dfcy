ABOUT THE SERVICE

A publishing workspace for independent creators.

Dingfeng Creator Studio is operated by {{ company_name }}. The service is available to creators who want a clear, creator-controlled way to transfer their original videos to their own TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok OAuth.
  2. The service retrieves the latest creator profile and available publishing settings.
  3. The creator selects and previews a video, edits the caption and chooses all available settings.
  4. Nothing is sent until the creator gives explicit consent and starts the transfer.
  5. The service displays TikTok's processing and publishing status.

Who can use it

The creator publishing workspace is intended for a broad audience of authentic TikTok creators. It is not restricted to Dingfeng employees, company-managed accounts or a private internal group.

Creator control

We do not choose a creator's account, add watermarks, prefill promotional captions, or publish without an explicit user action. Each user can disconnect their TikTok account at any time.
