Mode one

Upload to drafts

Transfer the prepared video to the creator's TikTok inbox so they can finish or publish in TikTok.

  • The creator reviews the video first
  • The destination account is shown clearly
  • Final publishing remains in TikTok
Mode two

Direct post

Publish through the connected account after the creator selects every available setting and confirms the action.

  • Privacy has no preselected value
  • Comment, Duet and Stitch are opt-in
  • Processing status remains visible
Before a post can move

A review checklist the creator can see.

  • The connected account identity is displayed.
  • Video preview and editable caption are visible.
  • Privacy is selected manually.
  • Interaction controls are off until enabled.
  • Commercial content is disclosed when applicable.
  • Music use is acknowledged before posting.
